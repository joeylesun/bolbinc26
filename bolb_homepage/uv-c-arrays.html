UV-C array products

UV-C arrays

Arrays combine multiple UV-C emitters into repeatable optical and mechanical formats, helping product teams scale irradiance, shape coverage, and simplify the transition from component testing to system prototypes.

PRODUCT FAMILYUV-C arrays
Product story

Scale optical power without losing sight of uniformity.

An array is more than a collection of LEDs. Its geometry, electrical grouping, board material, thermal path, and optical spacing must work together with the treatment chamber.

01 — Geometry

Choose the irradiation pattern

Linear, square, circular, and custom layouts support different ducts, reactors, apertures, and treatment footprints.

02 — Power and control

Group emitters into a manageable electrical architecture

Series/parallel organization, driver channels, monitoring, and dimming influence reliability, serviceability, and control resolution.

03 — Thermal uniformity

Keep every emitter inside its operating window

Board spreading, heatsink contact, interface material, airflow, and mounting pressure affect array-level output and lifetime.

Integration path

From irradiance map to integrated array.

1

Define the target field

Set treatment area, working distance, uniformity target, total optical output, and allowable shadow zones.

2

Select array geometry and drive topology

Choose emitter count, pitch, board outline, optical accessories, electrical grouping, and sensors.

3

Validate distribution and temperature

Map irradiance and temperature across the array under representative mounting and duty-cycle conditions.

Array-level inputs that determine useful optical performance.

Emitter pitch

Spacing, working distance, overlap, and irradiance uniformity across the active area.

↯
Electrical grouping

Series/parallel topology, driver channels, fault behavior, dimming, and monitoring.

▱
Board construction

MCPCB or other substrate, copper spreading, dielectric, connectors, and mounting points.

△
Array cooling

Heatsink flatness, interface material, airflow, liquid cooling, and hotspot management.
