UV-C LED products

UV-C LEDs

The semiconductor emitter is the foundation of every solid-state UV-C system. Bolb UV-C LEDs give product teams a compact source that can be arranged around the optical, electrical, and thermal requirements of the final equipment.

Product story

A compact source with system-level consequences.

This draft frames the LED as an engineering platform rather than a standalone specification. Final product claims should be tied to current datasheets and controlled test conditions.

01 — Source selection

Start with wavelength and optical output

Choose an emitter family based on target wavelength, required irradiance, package footprint, beam geometry, and expected operating conditions.

02 — Drive and thermal design

Convert electrical input into stable UV-C output

Drive current, junction temperature, mounting method, heatsinking, and duty cycle determine how the LED performs over time.

03 — Optical integration

Place photons where the application needs them

Emitter spacing, lenses, reflectors, window materials, and treatment-zone geometry turn a component into a usable dose field.

Integration path

From component requirement to validated optical source.

1

Define the source requirement

Set target wavelength, optical power, treatment geometry, duty cycle, and environmental limits.

2

Integrate package and thermal path

Design PCB, solder interface, heatsink, driver, optics, and monitoring around the selected LED.

3

Characterize in the real assembly

Measure radiant output, irradiance distribution, temperature, and stability in the actual product architecture.
