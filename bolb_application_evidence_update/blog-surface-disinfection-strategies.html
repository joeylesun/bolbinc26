Surface application guide

Compact fixture or AI-controlled zones?

The right UV-C surface-disinfection architecture depends first on the size of the space, the movement of people, and the number of treatment zones that must be supervised.

Draft technical perspective · Bolb application engineering

The finished systems discussed here are customer or partner implementation examples. Bolb supplies the LEDs, modules, arrays, and engineering support.

The central distinctionA compact fixture supervises one local light cone. A large-space control platform supervises many independent fixtures on a calibrated facility map.

1. Compact targeted treatment

The AdaptUV Targeted Air & Surface fixture is intended for smaller, clearly bounded spaces. It combines a 265 nm source, local radar/PIR presence sensing, an automatic iris, and visible cycle indicators in one device.

This architecture is appropriate when one ceiling-mounted or tabletop unit can cover the intended treatment area. Typical examples include offices, conference rooms, consultation rooms, hotel rooms, reception areas, and public restrooms.

Design questions

  • Does the defined light cone cover the required surfaces?
  • Where are the lowest-dose points and persistent shadows?
  • Does the sensor field cover every path into the treatment zone?
  • How long must the space remain clear to complete the required cycle?

2. AI-coordinated large spaces

A warehouse, shopping mall, airport, or large public facility may contain many UV-C fixtures and multiple pedestrian routes. In this case, one local sensor cannot represent the occupancy of the full environment.

The current SUVOS engineering architecture uses multiple camera feeds and a YOLOv8 human detector. Calibration converts camera pixels into common floor coordinates. The software then checks each detected person against configured UV-C zones and updates each fixture’s WAIT, CLEAN, or STANDBY state independently.

What the software contributes

  • Multi-camera observations mapped into one floor coordinate system.
  • Configurable treatment-zone position, radius, and cycle timing.
  • Independent state control so an occupied zone can stop without treating the entire building as occupied.
  • A local browser dashboard carrying people positions, zone state, occupancy, and remaining timer data.
  • A local SQLite record of ON and OFF state transitions.
  • Adaptive inference scheduling that increases the safety sampling rate while UV-C is active.
Engineering-build noteThe uploaded software is an internal development build. Final commercial copy should not state detection accuracy, certified response latency, production camera count, or formal fail-safe compliance until those items are validated and approved.

3. What both systems still require

Neither sensing architecture establishes microbial performance by itself. The installed system must still be measured at the real surfaces, including the lowest-dose locations, and tested under representative geometry, occupancy, environmental, and material conditions.

Selection summary

  • Choose a compact targeted architecture when one compact treatment zone can be supervised locally.
  • Choose a multi-zone control architecture when multiple fixtures must respond independently to movement across a larger floor plan.
  • Validate both for delivered dose, surface compatibility, access control, and fault response.
