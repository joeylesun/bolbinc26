Surface application

Targeted UV-C where contact happens.

UV-C LEDs can be integrated into cabinets, equipment interiors, mobile devices, and controlled fixtures to deliver a defined dose to high-touch or process surfaces.

View draft parameters →Discuss this application
Application story

Coverage—not only output—sets performance.

This draft explains how Bolb’s UV-C LED platform could fit into the complete product architecture. Final language should be supported by application-specific test data.

01 — The challenge

The challenge

Real surfaces include corners, texture, shadowing, variable distance, and materials with different UV reflectance. A single center-point irradiance value does not describe the entire treated area.

02 — The UV-C role

The UV-C role

Wide-angle emitters, arrays, reflectors, and multiple viewing angles are used to improve uniformity. The product controls exposure time and prevents access while the treatment cycle is active.

03 — The system story

The system story

The team maps the target surface, defines the minimum acceptable dose, selects emitter placement, and measures the lowest-dose region. Interlocks, timers, and presence controls are added according to the product’s safety architecture.

Development path

From requirement to validated system.

1

Map the treatment area

Define dimensions, materials, shadow zones, access conditions, and required cycle time.

2

Create uniform coverage

Combine emitter beam pattern, distance, reflectors, and multiple source positions.

3

Control and verify

Use interlocks or presence detection, monitor output, and validate dose at the worst-case location.
