Guardian Vision

Guardian Vision

Guardian Vision is a selected Bolb product for UV-C installations that benefit from presence-aware operating logic, coordinated cycle control, and a clearer connection between sensing and disinfection status.

View draft parameters →Request product information
PRODUCT FAMILYGuardian Vision
Product story

Add intelligent operating logic around the UV-C source.

Guardian Vision is one part of the wider product portfolio. It should be presented where sensing and control meaningfully improve the complete application—not as the company’s primary identity.

01 — Sense

Observe the defined treatment zone

A sensing layer monitors the operating area or access condition according to the selected installation architecture.

02 — Decide

Apply configurable safety and cycle logic

The controller interprets presence, access, dwell time, system state, and fault conditions before allowing or interrupting UV-C output.

03 — Coordinate

Connect status to the rest of the product

Drivers, indicators, alarms, logs, and supervisory controls can be coordinated so the system communicates what it is doing and why.

Integration path

From safety requirement to verified control behavior.

1

Define the protected zone and states

Map access points, expected users, treatment cycles, standby conditions, and failure responses.

2

Integrate sensing and control interfaces

Connect detection inputs, UV-C drivers, indicators, interlocks, communication, and local firmware logic.

3

Verify normal and fault scenarios

Test entry, exit, occlusion, sensor failure, power interruption, restart, maintenance, and manual override behavior.
