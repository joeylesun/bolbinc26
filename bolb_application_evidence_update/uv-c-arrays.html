InterfaceQuestions to settleVerification
ElectricalString topology, current regulation, power input, connector, protection, monitoringCurrent sharing, transient response, fault behavior, and driver thermal performance
ThermalHeat load, MCPCB interface, heatsink, airflow or liquid cooling, orientationCase and junction-temperature estimate across ambient and life
OpticalWorking distance, beam overlap, lenses or reflectors, uniformity, material transmissionCalibrated irradiance map at the application plane
MechanicalMounting flatness, fasteners, tolerances, service access, contamination protectionAssembly repeatability and optical alignment
