BOLB COMPONENT-LED WEBSITE RESTRUCTURE
=======================================

This package implements the strategy change that Bolb sells UV-C LEDs, modules, arrays, and engineering support. Complete fixtures and systems are presented only as customer or partner implementation examples.

Key changes
-----------
- Homepage is component-led, with product platform before applications.
- Products page contains only packaged LEDs, modules, arrays, custom solutions, and reference designs.
- New Powered by Bolb page separates customer systems from Bolb products.
- Air, Water, Surface, and Food Safety pages begin with the engineering problem and relevant Bolb components.
- Upper-room, targeted air/surface, B-W10T, and SUVOS are clearly labeled as customer implementations or architectures.
- Cross-links now connect application needs to actual Bolb components.
- Chatbot language recommends LEDs/modules and explains ownership of finished systems.
- Guardian Vision URL is retained as a migration page so older links do not fail.

Commercial/product content
--------------------------
Public product summaries were checked against Bolb's public website in July 2026. Before publication, confirm controlled datasheets, current bins, availability, lifetime, compliance scope, optical output, and exact product names.

----------------
