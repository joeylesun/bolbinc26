BOLB APPLICATION-PAGE EVIDENCE UPDATE

Strategy implemented
--------------------
Bolb is positioned as the supplier of UV-C LEDs, modules, arrays, and integration support. Complete fixtures/reactors/control systems are presented only as third-party customer or partner reference implementations.

Claim framework
---------------
1. Component capability: wavelength, optical output, package/module format, thermal/electrical requirements.
2. Complete-system evidence: each numerical result is labeled as tested, customer-reported, calculated, or adjacent evidence.
3. Claim boundary: organism, geometry, flow/room size, exposure time, and evidence source remain attached to the number.
4. OEM responsibility: final dose distribution, safety, certification, microbial testing, warranty, and system support belong to the complete-system manufacturer.

Application-page changes
------------------------
AIR
- Added a prominent explanation of Bolb versus system-builder responsibility.
- Upper-room and targeted fixtures are labeled reference implementations.
- Added customer-reported/calculated performance cards with conditions.

WATER

SURFACES

FOOD SAFETY
- Added adjacent water-system evidence but clearly states it is not a food-product claim.
- Added a component-to-system pathway and the minimum evidence required for a food-specific efficacy statement.

Publication controls
--------------------
Before release, obtain customer/partner permission to use names, images, documents, and performance data. Verify all numbers against controlled reports. Replace generic phrases such as kill rate with microbial reduction or deactivation and always preserve the conditions.
