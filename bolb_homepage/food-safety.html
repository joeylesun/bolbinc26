Food-safety application

Add a non-chemical control step to the line.

UV-C LEDs can be integrated around conveyors, packaging stations, storage zones, and equipment surfaces to supplement a broader food-safety program without leaving a chemical residue.

View draft parameters →Discuss this application
Application story

A dose matched to product and process.

This draft explains how Bolb’s UV-C LED platform could fit into the complete product architecture. Final language should be supported by application-specific test data.

01 — The challenge

The challenge

Food products vary in shape, color, moisture, surface texture, and UV sensitivity. Line speed and product orientation can create uneven exposure.

02 — The UV-C role

The UV-C role

Linear arrays and distributed emitters create treatment zones above, below, or around the process path. UV-C may target packaging, conveyor surfaces, water, or the food surface itself depending on the validated use case.

03 — The system story

The system story

A pilot starts with the microbial target and quality limits, then evaluates optical dose, line speed, temperature, product appearance, and shelf-life. The final system is validated as one control within the overall sanitation plan.

From requirement to validated system.
