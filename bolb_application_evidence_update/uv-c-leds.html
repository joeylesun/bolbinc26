Design workflow

Treat the LED as an electro-optical and thermal device.

1

Define the optical requirement

Target wavelength, delivered irradiance, dose, working distance, beam field, material transmission, and measurement plane.

2

Set the electrical and thermal point

Drive current, forward-voltage range, current regulation, junction-temperature target, heatsink, and interface resistance.

3

Validate in the real geometry

Measure output, spatial uniformity, temperature, degradation, and application performance under representative conditions.
