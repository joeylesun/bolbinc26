Air application

Cleaner air starts inside the airflow.

UV-C LEDs can be placed where air already moves—inside ducts, recirculating units, and compact air-treatment chambers—to apply a repeatable germicidal dose without adding chemicals to the airstream.

Application story

A controlled dose during every pass.

This draft explains how Bolb’s UV-C LED platform could fit into the complete product architecture. Final language should be supported by application-specific test data.

01 — The challenge

The challenge

Air moves quickly, organisms vary in susceptibility, and a compact chamber may provide only a short exposure time. The design must create sufficient irradiance across the active airflow while managing pressure drop, heat, and electrical power.

02 — The UV-C role

The UV-C role

High-output LEDs and arrays illuminate the treatment zone as air passes through it. Reflective chamber surfaces, emitter placement, and residence time work together to improve dose uniformity.

03 — The system story

The system story

A product team starts with target airflow and reduction goals, selects an emitter or array format, models the optical field, then validates performance in the actual duct or appliance. Guardian Vision may be added only when an occupied-space product needs presence-aware control.

Development path

From requirement to validated system.

1

Define the air path

Map airflow rate, chamber dimensions, residence time, target organism, and required reduction.

2

3

Validate the complete unit

Measure irradiance, temperature, airflow impact, and microbial reduction under representative conditions.

Key design inputs

Parameters that determine the complete system.

Airflow

LPM or CFM, velocity profile, turbulence, and residence time.

◌
Optical dose

Irradiance distribution multiplied by exposure time for each air parcel.

✦
Thermal design

Junction temperature, heatsink resistance, fan conditions, and driver efficiency.

◇
Validation

Microbial test method, sampling location, single-pass versus cumulative performance.
